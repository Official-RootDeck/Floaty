Privacy Policy for Floaty

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Floaty is an Android screenshot application developed by RootDeck.

Information We Collect

Floaty does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with RootDeck. The app does not require you to create an account.

Screenshots and Files

Screenshots are created at your request and are saved on your device, subject to the storage location and permissions selected in Android. RootDeck does not receive or access your screenshots.

Permissions

Floaty may request Android permissions or special access needed to capture screenshots and save them to your device. These permissions are used to provide the app's screenshot functionality.

Data Sharing

Floaty does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Floaty is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact RootDeck through the RootDeck GitHub organization.
