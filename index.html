Privacy Policy for Floaty

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Floaty is an Android screenshot and screen-recording application developed by RootDeck.

Information We Collect

Floaty does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with RootDeck. The app does not require you to create an account.

Screenshots and Files

Screenshots and screen recordings are created only when you initiate capture or recording and are saved locally on your device or to a folder you select. RootDeck does not receive these media files. If you enable microphone recording, audio is captured into the recording you requested and remains in the selected local destination.

Permissions

Floaty requests overlay, Accessibility, MediaProjection, storage-folder, and optional microphone permissions or special access as needed for its features. Accessibility is used to initiate screenshot capture at your request; MediaProjection is used for screen capture and recording. Microphone access is optional and used only when you enable audio recording. If you use filename variables that include the foreground app name or package, that information is read locally to name the screenshot file and is not sent to RootDeck.

Data Sharing

Floaty does not sell personal information. When you check for updates or download an update, the app connects to GitHub to retrieve release information and the APK. GitHub may receive standard connection information under its own privacy policy. Floaty does not upload screenshots, recordings, or microphone audio.

Children's Privacy

Floaty is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact RootDeck through the RootDeck GitHub organization.
